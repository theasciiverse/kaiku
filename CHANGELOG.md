# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)

The first version of Kaiku: Echoes Home.

### The game
- A sideways shoot-'em-up with typing: blasted deep into enemy space, fight
  your way through two hostile fleets, decode garbled echoes of Earth's
  transmissions by typing them, and take the right gate home.
- **Echoes** flicker between noise and clear; the true one is the clearest,
  and clearer the nearer home. Lock on with a letter (ZType-style), and the
  next letter you need always shows. The receiver analyses each decoded echo
  and rules out the jammed ones.
- **Three gates** end every sector, and only one leads nearer home. A wrong
  gate is a detour, never game over. The field swings round through a gate,
  and a route map shows the way home.
- **The war:** two fleets that fight each other as much as you. Scouts,
  gunships, interceptors, mines, lancers, jammers and carriers, more of them
  sector by sector; a carrier guards the gates every third sector; tunnel
  walls from sector 2.
- **Power-ups:** word pods you type, and drops from ships you shoot down
  (power-ups and shield cells) that you fly into. Spread shot, rapid fire,
  shield, smart bombs, an escort drone, slow time.
- **Dash**, kill chains up to ×8, extra lives, a run summary and a saved high
  score.
- **The opening:** a Metroid-style story crawl, the controls, then a cockpit
  scene where you repair the receiver (the tutorial), with the war far off
  in the window.
- **Home:** Earth rises, the last echo plays clear, Earth's fighters fly out
  and form up around your ship, and a closing crawl welcomes you home a hero.
  Then how the run went, and the credits.

### Two players
- Plug in a gamepad: one flies on the pad (the pilot), the other types on the
  keys (comms). Either can also do it all alone. The pad rumbles.

### Sound
- All synthesised in the game: effects for every moment, and music that
  turns from chiptune into dark space ambient over the journey, with drums
  that follow the fighting and a theme heard in pieces until home.

### Terminal
- Exact key presses and releases with the kitty keyboard protocol; timing
  and latched modes elsewhere. ASCII, Braille and half-block graphics.
  F1 shows the controls.
- Flags: `--skip-intro`, `--sector N`, `--demo`, `--credits`, `--mute`,
  `--no-music`, `--version`.
