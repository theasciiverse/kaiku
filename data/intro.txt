AN INTERPLANETARY WAR
RAGES BETWEEN TWO FLEETS.

ON THE LONG RUN HOME,
A SURGE TORE THROUGH
YOUR SHIP AND FLUNG IT
FAR OFF COURSE, INTO A
POCKET OF SPACE THAT NO
CHART HAS EVER KNOWN.

YOUR RECEIVER STILL
CATCHES FAINT ECHOES:
EARTH'S OLD VOICES,
BOUNCING IN THE DARK.

THE CLEARER THE ECHO,
THE NEARER HOME.

FOLLOW THEM.
