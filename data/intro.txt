AN INTERGALACTIC WAR
BURNS ACROSS THE STARS,
AND YOU ARE IN THE
THICK OF IT.

THEN A SAVAGE BLAST
TEARS THROUGH YOUR SHIP
AND HURLS IT FAR BEYOND
THE FRONT LINE, DEEP INTO
ENEMY SPACE WHERE NO
HUMAN HAS EVER BEEN.

YOUR RECEIVER STILL
CATCHES FAINT ECHOES:
EARTH'S OLD VOICES,
BOUNCING IN THE DARK.

THE CLEARER THE ECHO,
THE NEARER HOME.

FOLLOW THEM.
